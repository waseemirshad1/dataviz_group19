### CV Idea: Skill Flower Diagram (inspired by p.2)

- **What it visualizes:** Multiple professional competency areas as petals radiating from a center. Each petal represents one domain: psychology, sales, software development, data science, machine learning. Petal size encodes self-assessed proficiency or years of experience in that domain.
- **How it works:** Modeled on the Bloemen-diagram from p.2, where each petal = one electricity activity and petal size = energy use. Here, each petal = one skill domain and petal size = proficiency/experience level. The flower form makes the profile immediately scannable and visually distinctive — a recruiter remembers it.
- **Adaptation for our context:** The transition from psychology + sales + software dev toward data science / ML is shown by having the data science and ML petals be the largest, while the others are medium-sized (showing background, not absence). The center could encode overall years of professional experience as a circle size. A second, smaller flower could show tool-level skills (Python, R, SQL, Tableau, etc.) as a companion visual.
- **Page reference:** p.2 (Bloemen-diagram / Flower diagram)

### CV Idea: Cumulative Experience Timeline (inspired by p.1)

- **What it visualizes:** Professional experience accumulated over time as a cumulative area or line chart. X-axis = time (years); Y-axis = cumulative "experience units" (could be months in role, projects completed, or simply a running total of roles). Multiple lines or stacked areas for different domains (psychology, sales, software, data science).
- **How it works:** Modeled on the cumulative consumption line chart (p.1) where lines never decrease and slope encodes rate. Here, steep rises = active periods of learning/work in a domain; plateaus = career pivots away from that area. The chart shows the full arc of the career trajectory and makes the pivot toward data science visible as a rising line overtaking earlier domains.
- **Adaptation for our context:** Start the timeline at the beginning of the career. Show the psychology and sales lines rising early, then flattening. Show the software development line rising next, then the data science line accelerating most steeply toward the present. The ML6 internship would appear as the steepest recent rise in the data science line — visually emphasizing the current trajectory.
- **Page reference:** p.1 (Geaccumuleerde Verbruikslijnen / cumulative lines)

### CV Idea: Mirror Chart — Technical Skills vs. Human Skills (inspired by p.1)

- **What it visualizes:** A diverging/mirror layout with time on the vertical axis (career timeline). Left side = "human-facing" skills (psychology, communication, sales, client management). Right side = "technical" skills (programming, data analysis, ML). Bar lengths encode proficiency or activity level in each domain at each career stage.
- **How it works:** Modeled on JA7 (p.1), the mirror chart that shows commercial vs. residential energy use on opposite sides of a shared time axis. Here, the shared axis is the career timeline. The visual immediately communicates the dual-competency profile — strong on both sides — which is the key differentiator for a data science candidate with a psychology and sales background.
- **Adaptation for our context:** The left side shows psychology training and sales experience growing early in the career. The right side shows software development and data science growing later. The ML6 internship sits at the top of the right side. The visual argument: this candidate brings the full width of both sides, not just the technical right side.
- **Page reference:** p.1 (JA7 mirror chart)

### CV Idea: Climate Stripes — Learning Intensity Over Time (inspired by p.2)

- **What it visualizes:** Each stripe = one month or quarter of the career. Color encodes learning/upskilling intensity in data science: dark green = intensive learning period (bootcamp, major project, internship); dark gray = consolidation or off-domain period; white = neutral.
- **How it works:** Modeled on the climate stripes (Klimaatstrepen, p.2) that show deviation from average. Here, each stripe shows deviation from "baseline" activity level in the data science upskilling journey. The pattern communicates dedication and consistency of development without a single word.
- **Adaptation for our context:** The stripes would show an intensifying green pattern over the past 1–2 years as the pivot toward data science accelerates, culminating in the ML6 internship as the darkest green stripe. This is a minimalist, striking design element that could appear as a header bar or sidebar accent in a modern CV layout rather than as a standalone chart.
- **Page reference:** p.2 (Klimaatstrepen / climate stripes)
